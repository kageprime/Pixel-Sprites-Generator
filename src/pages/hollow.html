    How to Play

    The descent

    You carry one seal of 4 cracks. Cut Hosts to crack their seals, break them, and take their ash. Spend 2 ash on a Cinder Wheel. Veil through what you cannot block.

    Bindings

    WASD / arrows move · J cut chain · K wheel (2 ash) · Space veil · hold L guard · E rest / take · Q E camera · M mute · Esc pause. Touch: stick + A/B/X/Y, hold Guard.

    Signs

    <!> above a Host is unblockable — veil, don't cut. A pale ring is a rest seal. The way down opens when Pell is filed.
