How to Play

The bout

You are P1, blue. Break the rival's seal before yours cracks. Launch them skyward with K, meet them in the air, and drive them down.

Bindings

WASD / arrows move · Space jump · J / Z / click strike · K / X / right-click launcher, plunge in the air · Shift / V dash · hold L guard · Q E camera · T slow motion · B cycle skin · M mute · Esc menus

Seals

Hits crack the rival's seal ring. A cracked seal breaks open — juggle the pieces before they get up. Skins and the camera are chosen before the bout.
